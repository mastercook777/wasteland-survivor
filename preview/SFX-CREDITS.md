# Sound-effect credits

A28 uses compact CC0 recordings as the physical attack layer and adds locally
generated transients, mechanical clicks, low-frequency weight and tails at run
time. If a recording cannot be decoded, the procedural layer remains audible.

## Firearms

The five browser-ready blast layers below are CC0 excerpts of **The Free
Firearm Sound Library**, recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
Matthew Nanney. They were obtained from the public Fatal Funnel asset bundle,
whose licence register explicitly permits redistribution of these files.
